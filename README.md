# Decentralized Environmental Restoration Tracking

A blockchain-based platform for transparent, verifiable environmental restoration project management, enabling stakeholders to track ecological improvements, verify impacts, and ensure accountable distribution of conservation funding across global restoration initiatives.

## Overview

This decentralized system revolutionizes environmental restoration by creating immutable records of ecological recovery efforts, from reforestation and wetland restoration to marine conservation and biodiversity recovery. The platform ensures transparency, accountability, and scientific rigor in restoration projects while facilitating efficient funding distribution based on verified outcomes.

## System Architecture

The platform operates through five interconnected smart contracts that create a comprehensive environmental restoration management ecosystem:

### 1. Project Verification Contract
**Purpose**: Validates and certifies restoration initiatives
- Project proposal evaluation and scientific review
- Methodology validation and best practice compliance
- Stakeholder verification and capability assessment
- Regulatory compliance and permit tracking

**Key Features**:
- Multi-tier scientific peer review process
- Integration with conservation standards (VCS, Gold Standard, Plan Vivo)
- Real-time permit and regulatory status monitoring
- Stakeholder credential verification and experience tracking
- Risk assessment and project feasibility analysis

### 2. Baseline Assessment Contract
**Purpose**: Records comprehensive pre-restoration environmental conditions
- Biodiversity surveys and species inventories
- Soil, water, and air quality measurements
- Carbon stock assessments and calculations
- Ecosystem service valuations

**Key Features**:
- Standardized environmental monitoring protocols
- Integration with satellite imagery and remote sensing data
- IoT sensor network for continuous environmental monitoring
- Species identification through AI and machine learning
- Geospatial mapping and GIS integration
- Historical data analysis and trend identification

### 3. Progress Monitoring Contract
**Purpose**: Tracks restoration milestones and implementation activities
- Real-time progress updates and milestone verification
- Resource deployment and activity logging
- Timeline adherence and schedule management
- Quality assurance and adaptive management

**Key Features**:
- Automated milestone verification through IoT and satellite data
- Photographic and video documentation with timestamp verification
- Weather and climate condition tracking
- Resource utilization monitoring (seeds, equipment, labor)
- Adaptive management protocol implementation
- Community engagement and participation tracking

### 4. Impact Measurement Contract
**Purpose**: Quantifies and verifies ecological improvements
- Biodiversity recovery metrics and species population tracking
- Carbon sequestration measurement and verification
- Ecosystem service enhancement quantification
- Water and soil quality improvement assessment

**Key Features**:
- Standardized impact measurement methodologies
- Integration with environmental monitoring systems
- Machine learning-based trend analysis and prediction
- Third-party verification and validation protocols
- Long-term impact tracking and sustainability assessment
- Co-benefits quantification (social, economic, health)

### 5. Funding Allocation Contract
**Purpose**: Manages transparent distribution of restoration resources
- Performance-based funding release mechanisms
- Multi-source funding pool management
- Automated payment triggers based on verified outcomes
- Financial transparency and audit trails

**Key Features**:
- Milestone-based payment automation
- Multi-stakeholder funding coordination
- Impact-based bonus and penalty systems
- Real-time budget tracking and expense management
- Currency hedging and exchange rate management
- Fraud prevention and financial security measures

- Baseline environmental assessment data
- Regulatory permits and compliance documentation

- Funding commitments or fundraising strategy

### Implementation Steps
1. **Project Registration**: Submit proposal through Project Verification Contract
2. **Baseline Documentation**: Record initial conditions via Baseline Assessment Contract
3. **Monitoring Setup**: Configure tracking parameters in Progress Monitoring Contract
4. **Impact Framework**: Establish measurement criteria in Impact Measurement Contract
5. **Funding Configuration**: Set up resource allocation in Funding Allocation Contract
6. **Implementation Launch**: Begin restoration activities with automated tracking

### Core Endpoints
```

GET /api/v1/baseline/{projectId} - Retrieve baseline assessment data
POST /api/v1/monitoring/update - Submit progress monitoring data
GET /api/v1/impact/{projectId}/metrics - Retrieve impact measurements
PUT /api/v1/funding/release - Trigger milestone-based payment
```
